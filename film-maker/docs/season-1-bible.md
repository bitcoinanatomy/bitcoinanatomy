# Season 1 — *Unspent* · series bible (v3)

Decided in the v3 grill session, 2026-10-26. Earlier drafts are archived at `project/archive/` (v1 *Into the Protocol*, v2 *The Small Light*). The story itself is in `scripts/seed-story.ts`; review it on the **Story arcs** page (plot overview) and the **World map** page.

## Premise
Two coins born from nothing inside a miner's node have a hundred blocks to wait before they're real, while a rival builds a chain in the dark that could erase them.

Precedents: *Once Upon a Time… Life* (protocol parts as workers), *Inside Out* (a human's choices drive the world inside), *Soul* (two worlds and a crossing), *Powers of Ten* (scale moves).

## The two worlds
- **Bitcoin world.** Each node is a huge sphere with a city of light inside, where the crew works.
  - **The core** sits at its centre. It holds the timechain helix and is also the **portal**.
  - **The network void** is the black space between nodes, strung with other nodes (explorer network page).
  - Every node holds the same coins.
  - The story follows the miner's node.
- **Human world.** A small river town at night. Ana's garage sits at its edge, powered by a little dam.
- **Crossing.** You go through the core, which scales up to an infinite wall. On the other side you're in the human world. There, coins and crew are **invisible passengers** inside what Bitcoin really is out there: the phone's glow, the rig's hum, a QR code on paper. Humans never see them.
- **Look.** There is no real colour anywhere; we see everything through value and energy.
  - The Bitcoin world is bright monochrome with white surfaces, brighter where there's value (coins).
  - The human world is mostly dark. Only Bitcoin things give off light: particles, a few or many.
  - When a node goes down, its machinery stops and no data moves. Only the coins keep their light.
- **Coins vs crew.**
  - **Coins (UTXOs)** are glowing white spheres with big heads; brighter and bigger = more value.
  - **Crew** are program parts, not coins: matte graphite bodies, flat machine heads (box or cylinder) and one glowing tool or badge each. Validator crest, Hasher visor, Clerk spectacles, Gossip antennae, Host slab.
- **Visual language** follows the explorer:

  | Thing | Looks like |
  |---|---|
  | Block | Glass cube with a header bar |
  | Timechain | Descending helix of discs |
  | Mempool | Spiral of slabs |
  | Node | Membrane sphere |
  | Network | Dots and faint links |
  | Coin | Glowing sphere, size by value |

## Cast
| | Who | Want / role |
|---|---|---|
| **Sati** | Coinbase output, a few thousand sats: the smallest coin the chain will carry | Wants to **be spent**, to matter and go somewhere, at the cost of losing herself |
| **Bit** | One-bitcoin output from the same coinbase; huge, brightest | Patient and protective. Secretly wants **Lightning speed** (season 2) |
| **Coinbase** | Silent giant, born from a valid block, from nothing | Never speaks. Looks around once, tenderly, and breaks into its outputs. The ancient frozen coins are other coinbases that never broke apart: sleeping giants with lost keys |
| **Ana** | The miner = keyholder = node runner, in the garage | Her choices (hold, spend, lose power, almost get phished) land as fate inside |
| **Crew of six** | The Node (voice of the sphere), Validator, Gossip, Hasher, Mempool Host, Script Clerk | They live in the miner's node and go dark with it. Coins persist; crew don't. After a reboot they wake groggy and resync |
| **The Rewriter** | Villain: a rival miner's hidden, longer chain | A coinbase can't move for 100 blocks: the season's countdown. Outside, he's a big operation that tries to buy Ana |
| **Pip** | A tiny, funny coin in a low-fee transaction | Sati's friend. His block is the one orphaned in Ep7 |
| **The Counterfeiter** | Coins from nothing | Rejected by every node (Ep4) |
| **The Thief** | Phishes Ana outside; his agent fails inside without her signature | Ep6 |
| **Mili** | A millisat that lives in Lightning | **Season 2.** A spark in Ep8's last shot. She can only reach the chain by merging into a bigger coin when a channel closes |

Loss is the season's second antagonist: dark nodes, lost keys, frozen ancient coins.

## Rules of the story
- **Fungibility.**
  - We foreshadow it in Ep2: strangers melt into a transaction and come out as new coins with no memory.
  - Sati chooses it in Ep8. The coin that comes out is new, with one small **echo** of her (a hum).
- **No lectures.**
  - No narrator.
  - One mechanism per episode, shown visually.
  - The crew explain only as shop talk while they work. Sati and Bit learn by watching and by asking the Node, who answers in one short line.
  - At most one line of real explanation per scene.
- **Rhythm.** Wordless reaction and insert shots, L-cuts, and silences where the music would explain.
- **Music.** Every cue shares the season motif seed, so the same tune comes back in new keys and modes.
- **Length.** About 12–15 minutes per episode, 8 episodes. Leave room for characters to talk and get to know each other: banter, likes, fears, naming.

## Episodes
1. **Coinbase.**
   - Cold open: a wordless Powers of Ten fall from the dark town, into the garage, through the node's LED, and into the city of light.
   - Cubes shuffle for the nonce, then click: a block. The Coinbase rises and breaks apart.
   - Sati and Bit wake, test their bodies (Bit is too loud), and learn they're locked to the miner and must wait a hundred blocks.
   - The Clerk reads their values (3,200 sats; one bitcoin), and they **name each other**: Sati, Bit.
   - On the crew's rounds, the Gossip passes on a rumour of a node up north losing power.
   - The Giant's Face: Sati realises the giant didn't vanish, it turned into them.
   - Night Talk: likes and fears. Bit wants to be fast once and dreads being spent; Sati dreads staying forever.
   - Ana phones her mum: she's keeping the block; if the dam holds through winter she can pay off the rigs.
   - Something dark is building blocks. Ana wakes to "block found."
2. **Transaction.** They watch strangers melt into a transaction (foreshadowing). A wrong key is turned away.
3. **The Mempool.** The waiting room. Sati befriends Pip, who finally makes it into the newest block.
4. **The Counterfeiter.** Coins from nothing; every node rejects him. He drifts toward the Rewriter.
5. **The Dark Node.**
   - A storm kills the node and the crew go dark.
   - Sati and Bit wake **outside** as spirits in the void and see themselves in every node.
   - The first crossing into the human world. Reboot and resync.
6. **The Thief.** Phished outside, blocked inside: no signature, no move. Ana almost types her seed words, and doesn't.
7. **The Rewrite.**
   - Ana refuses the offer and mines through the storm.
   - Pip's block is orphaned.
   - Honest work outweighs the hidden chain, and Sati and Bit survive by depth. The dam is damaged.
8. **Spent.**
   - Block 100. Ana pays toward the dam repair.
   - Sati is spent, crossing as a passenger inside the payment. A light comes back on in town, and her echo hums.
   - Ana opens a channel with Bit, and Mili's spark races past.
